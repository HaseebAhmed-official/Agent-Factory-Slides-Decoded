# Slide 81: AI Agent Value Chain

## Core Message
**The Evolution of Value: From Commodity Intelligence to Managed Labor**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Value Chain Stages
1.  **Stage 1: Raw Intelligence (The Model):**
    *   *Input:* Data + Compute.
    *   *Value:* Low (Commodity).
2.  **Stage 2: Proprietary Expertise (The Skill):**
    *   *Input:* Human Domain Knowledge + SDD.
    *   **Value: High** (Unique IP).
3.  **Stage 3: Agent Orchestration (The Product):**
    *   *Input:* Multi-Agent workflows + Guardrails.
    *   *Value: Very High* (Solution-focused).
4.  **Stage 4: Digital FTE (The Service):**
    *   *Input:* Continuous operation + Accountability.
    *   **Value: Maximum** (Labor budget capture).

#### 2. The "Aha!" Moment
"Don't get stuck at Stage 1. Most people are just 'prompting models.' The goal is Stage 4: **Digital Labor as a Service**."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To guide entrepreneurs and engineers on where to focus their efforts to maximize profit margins and defensibility.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Using a model is easy; building a reliable worker is hard. Value increases as you get closer to the business outcome.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **Value Migration.** As "Raw Intelligence" becomes cheaper (token price wars), value migrates up to "Digital FTEs." This is similar to how the value of electricity moved from "Generation" to "Appliances" and "Industrial Automation." Defensibility lives in Stage 2 (Proprietary Skills) and Stage 4 (Deep integration into client workflows).

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Selling a ChatGPT wrapper (Stage 1).
*   **Intermediate:** Selling a "Lawyer's Prompt Pack" (Stage 2).
*   **PhD / Advanced:** Selling a "Digital Claims Adjuster" that is integrated into 5 major insurance databases and makes binding settlement decisions (Stage 4).

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Business Strategy:** Map your current product on this chain. If you are in Stage 1, plan your migration to Stage 2 immediately.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Margins:** Stage 1 has near-zero margins. Stage 4 has 80%+ margins.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Abstraction:** Each stage abstracts the one below it. The Digital FTE (Stage 4) abstracts the complex orchestration (Stage 3).

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Is your product 'intelligence' or 'work'?*
*   *What part of your value chain is impossible for OpenAI to replicate tomorrow?*
