### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To provide the "Business Case" for the CFO.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** It's cheaper and works longer.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The "Scaling" Asymmetry.** Hiring 10 humans takes 3 months. Spinning up 10 agents takes 3 seconds. This speed allows businesses to seize market opportunities instantly (e.g., "Respond to 10,000 leads today").

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Cheaper support agent.
*   **Intermediate:** 24/7 coverage without shift differentials.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Burst Capacity.** A retailer spins up 5,000 Digital FTEs for Cyber Monday and shuts them down on Tuesday. Impossible with humans.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Metric:** "Cost per Available Hour."

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Efficiency:** It fundamentally changes the unit economics of the firm.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Cost Monitor:** Real-time dashboard showing `Human Equivalent Cost` vs. `Actual Agent Cost`.
