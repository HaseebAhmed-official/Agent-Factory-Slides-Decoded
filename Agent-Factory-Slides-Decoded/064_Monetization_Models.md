# Slide 64: Monetizing Expertise: The 2026 Revenue Playbook

## Core Message
**Four Models for Selling Digital Labor**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The 4 Models
1.  **Digital FTE (Subscription):** Monthly fee for a "Teammate."
2.  **Success Fee (Outcome):** Pay per lead/sale.
3.  **License (IP):** Selling the Skill folder to enterprises.
4.  **Skill Marketplace:** Selling small "Expertise Packs" ($49).

#### 2. The Shift
"From selling **Software** to selling **Labor**."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To define the business model. Technology without a business model is a hobby.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Charge for value.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The "CapEx to OpEx" Shift.** Buying software is CapEx (Asset). Hiring labor is OpEx (Expense). Digital FTEs sit in the OpEx budget, which is often larger and easier to approve for managers.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** $10/month for "AI Writing Tool."
*   **Intermediate:** $2,000/month for "AI Content Manager" (FTE).
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Equity for Agents.** A model where the Agent takes 0% cash but 5% equity in the startup it helps build. (The DAO model).

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Billing Integration:** Stripe usage-based billing vs. flat-rate subscriptions.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Valuation:** FTE revenue is valued higher than "Tool" revenue because it is stickier. (You fire a tool, you don't fire a teammate).

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Metering Sidecar:** A proxy that counts tokens/tasks per tenant to calculate margins.

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you pricing like a tool ($20) or an employee ($2000)?*
