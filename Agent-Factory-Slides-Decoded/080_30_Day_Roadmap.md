# Slide 80: The "Agent Factory" Roadmap (30-Day Plan)

## Core Message
**From Learning to Earning: Your First Digital FTE in 4 Weeks**

### 1. Objective
To provide an actionable, time-boxed execution plan. This slide removes "Analysis Paralysis" and forces the audience to commit to a **Minimum Viable Agent (MVA)**.

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **Velocity over Perfection:** In AI, the market moves every 30 days. If your "Build" phase is 6 months, you are building for a dead market.
*   **The Learning Loop:** You don't know what agent to build until you try to sell one. This roadmap gets you to the "Selling" phase as fast as possible.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights (The 4 Weeks)
1.  **Week 1: Knowledge Extraction (The Gap):** Find a repetitive, high-value task. Record the human expert.
2.  **Week 2: The Blueprint (SDD):** Write the **Spec** and the **AGENTS.md**. Build the **Eval Suite**.
3.  **Week 3: Manufacturing (The Build):** Use Claude Code to generate the **Skill folder** and Python scripts. Pass the tests.
4.  **Week 4: Deployment & Capture (The Sell):** Deploy to a client (or your own team) and charge for the outcome.

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **Week 2 is the Pivot:** Most people skip Week 2 and go straight to Week 3 (Coding). This is why they fail. **The Spec is the product.** If the Spec is wrong, the agent is wrong.
*   **The MVA (Minimum Viable Agent):** Don't build a "CEO Agent." Build a "Weekly Sales Report Agent." Pick a task that takes a human 4 hours but can be done by an agent in 4 seconds.
*   **Monetization First:** In Week 1, you should already know *who* will pay for this. If you can't name 3 people who would pay $500/mo for this worker, pick a different task.

#### 4. When to Use?
*   **Startup Kickoff:** Your "Day 1" instruction manual.
*   **Intrapreneurship:** Pitching a 30-day "Pilot" to your boss.

#### 5. Examples

##### a. Basic (The 30-Day 'Email Agent')
*   *W1:* Record Support Rep answering common questions.
*   *W2:* Write a Spec for "Common Inquiry Classification."
*   *W3:* Claude writes the script to connect to Gmail and draft replies.
*   *W4:* Support Rep reviews drafts. Company saves 10 hours/week.

##### b. Intermediate (The 30-Day 'Lead Scraper')
*   *W1:* Interview Sales Lead on how they find prospects.
*   *W2:* Define "Ideal Customer Profile" (ICP) in a Markdown file.
*   *W3:* Build MCP for LinkedIn + Apollo.
*   *W4:* Sell "Lead List as a Service" to 5 clients.

##### c. PhD / Advanced (The Self-Building Studio)
*   *Concept:* **Recursive Roadmap Execution.**
*   *Scenario:* You use the first 30 days to build an agent whose only job is to **perform the 30-day roadmap** for *other* tasks. By Day 60, you have an agent that "Extracts," "Specifies," and "Hardens" new skills for you. You have automated the **Factory's own R&D**.

### 7. Why This Matters?
*   **Momentum:** Most people quit because they don't see progress. A 30-day win creates a "Belief System" in your team.

### 8. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **Scope Creep:** Forces you to focus on one role (FTE) instead of building a "God-bot."

### 9. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Iteration Cycle:** This isn't a one-time line; it's a **Loop**. Every 30 days, you should add a new "Skill" to your Factory's library.

### 10. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Trying to automate a task that hasn't been "Manualized" yet.
    *   *Correction:* If a human can't do it with a checklist, an AI can't do it with a Spec.
*   **Practice:** "The Friday Review." Every Friday, show what the agent built this week.
