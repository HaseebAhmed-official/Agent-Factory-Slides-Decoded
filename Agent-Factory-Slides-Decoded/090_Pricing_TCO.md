# Slide 90: Pricing & Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

## Core Message
**How to Price Digital Labor: Extreme Margins**

### 1. Objective
To establish the "Unit Economics" of a Digital FTE business. This slide explains why Agentic AI is the most profitable business model in history.

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **Value-Based Pricing vs Cost-Plus:** Traditional software is priced "Cost-Plus" ($10/mo). Digital Labor is priced "Value-Based" (relative to a human salary).
*   **The Margin Gap:** The cost of compute (Tokens) is dropping (Moore's Law). The cost of human labor is rising (Inflation). The margin between "Cost" and "Value" is expanding every year.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights
*   **Starter Role ($500 - $2,000 / mo):** Simple tasks (Scraping, sorting). Replaces an intern/VA.
*   **Specialist Role ($2,000 - $5,000 / mo):** Domain knowledge (Accounting, Support). Replaces a junior professional.
*   **Enterprise Role ($10,000+ / mo):** Complex orchestration, high security. Replaces a team or agency.

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **TCO (Total Cost of Ownership):**
    *   **Token Costs:** 10-20% of price.
    *   **Hosting/Ops:** 5-10% of price.
    *   **Net Margin:** **70-85%.**
*   **Token Arbitrage:** As you optimize your Skills (Slide 50), your token costs go *down* while your price stays the same. Efficiency gains flow directly to profit.
*   **Consumer Surplus Capture:** A human costs $5,000. You charge $2,000. The customer saves $3,000. You make $1,800 profit. Everyone wins (except the disrupted human).

### 4. When to Use?
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Determining your price points.
*   **Investor Relations:** Showing the "Unit Economics" slide in a pitch deck.

### 5. Examples

#### a. Basic (The Tool)
*   *Price:* $20/mo.
*   *Perception:* "Cheap Subscription." High Churn.

#### b. Intermediate (The Digital FTE)
*   *Price:* $1,500/mo.
*   *Perception:* "Cheap Employee." Low Churn.

#### c. PhD / Advanced (Dynamic ROI Pricing)
*   *Concept:* **Value-Capture Protocol.**
*   *Scenario:* An agent that monitors its own "Value Created" (e.g., money recovered in an audit). It bills the client 15% of that value automatically via a smart contract. The pricing is **Variable and Infinite**. If the agent finds $1M, you make $150k. If it finds $0, you make $0. Zero risk for the client, infinite upside for you.

### 6. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Billing:** Use Stripe "Subscriptions" for the base fee and "Usage Metering" for the variable costs (if passing tokens to client).

### 7. Why This Matters?
*   **Profitability:** This is why Agentic AI is the "Greatest Business Opportunity." The margins are higher than SaaS and the revenue is higher than Services.

### 8. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **The "Race to the Bottom":** Prevents you from competing on price with $10 tools. Move up market.

### 9. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Billing Sidecar:** A module that calculates the "Cost of Goods Sold" (COGS) for every agent run in real-time, alerting you if a specific customer becomes unprofitable (e.g., in a loop).

### 10. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Undervaluing the agent.
    *   *Correction:* Do not price based on your cost. Price based on the *Human Equivalent*.
*   **Practice:** "Anchor Pricing." Show the cost of the Human FTE ($5k) next to the Digital FTE ($2k) on the pricing page.

### 11. Reflection Questions
1.  *Are you pricing like a 'Utility' (Cost-plus) or a 'Partner' (Value-based)?*
2.  *Do you know your profit margin per agent?*
3.  *Could you raise your price 10x if you changed the positioning from 'Tool' to 'Employee'?*
