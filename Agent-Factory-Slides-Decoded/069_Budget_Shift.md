# Slide 69: Budget Shift

## Core Message
**Tapping into Headcount Budgets**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Insight
*   **IT Budget:** Small. (Used for Slack, Zoom).
*   **Headcount Budget:** Massive. (Used for Salaries).

#### 2. The Strategy
"Don't fight for the IT budget. Sell a 'Digital Senior Developer' and tap into the **Headcount Budget**. Charge $2,000/mo instead of $20/mo."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To fix the "Pricing Error" most AI startups make.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Salaries > Subscriptions.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The "OpEx" Hack.** Managers have "Open Headcount." If they can't find a human (Talent Shortage), they can use that budget to hire a Digital FTE. You are solving their *hiring problem*, not their *software problem*.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Selling a tool for $50.
*   **Intermediate:** Selling a service for $500.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Performance-Based Comp.** "This agent takes 10% of the money it saves you." If it saves $1M (Headcount), you get $100k. This aligns incentives perfectly.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Sales Pitch:** Speak to HR/Ops, not just IT.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Revenue:** It is the difference between a $1M ARR company and a $100M ARR company.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **Reporting:** The agent must generate "Timesheets" or "Activity Logs" to justify its "Salary."

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Which budget line item are you targeting?*
