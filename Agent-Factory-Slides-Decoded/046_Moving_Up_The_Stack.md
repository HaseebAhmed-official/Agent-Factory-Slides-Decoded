# Slide 46: Moving Up The Stack

## Core Message
**Where is the Value? Competing at the Skill Layer.**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Value Pyramid
*   **Models (Bottom):** Commodity. Everyone has GPT-4.
*   **Agents (Middle):** Emerging Standard.
*   **Skills (Top):** **Proprietary Logic.** The Winning Layer.

#### 2. The Insight
"Don't compete at the Model level. Compete at the Skill level. This is where your unique domain expertise (law, medicine) allows you to win."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To guide business strategy. Identifying where "Alpha" (excess return) comes from in the AI era.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Don't build LLMs. Use them. Build *Guidelines* for them.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The Thin Wrapper Problem.** Apps that just wrap GPT-4 are dead. Apps that wrap **Unique Data + Unique Workflows (Skills)** are defensible. The "Skill" is the crystallization of your business secret sauce.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Building a "Chat with PDF" app (Commodity).
*   **Intermediate:** Building a "Legal Contract Reviewer" with proprietary prompts (Better).
*   **PhD / Advanced:** Building a "Network of Agents" that simulates a specific market sector using proprietary historical data and behavioral skills (Monopoly).

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Focus:** Spend 80% of time on `SKILL.md` / `tools`, 20% on infra.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Investment:** Investors want to know "What is your Moat?" The answer is "My Library of Specialized Skills."

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **Abstraction Layers:** We abstract away the Model so we can focus on the Logic.

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you building a commodity (Wrapper) or a proprietary asset (Skill)?*
