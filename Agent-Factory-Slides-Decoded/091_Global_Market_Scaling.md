# Slide 91: How to Reach the Global Market?

## Core Message
**Scaling and Distribution in the Agentic Era**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Scaling Opportunity
*   **Topic:** Moving from "One Agent" to "Global Workforce."
*   **Goal:** Reaching millions of users with near-zero marginal effort.

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To outline the distribution strategy for Digital FTEs.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Put your agent where the people are.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The Platform Play.** You don't build a sales team; you build a **Distribution Engine**. By leveraging existing ecosystems (OpenAI, Slack, Microsoft), you inherit their trust and user base. **Localization.** In the Agent era, "Global" is the default. A Skill written in English can be "Compiled" by the Agent Factory into 50 languages instantly.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Posting a link on Twitter.
*   **Intermediate:** Listing on the OpenAI GPT Store.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Self-Propagating Agents.** Agents that identify potential new customers (via LinkedIn/Web), reach out to them, offer a free trial of *themselves*, and onboard the customer autonomously. The Product is its own Salesperson.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Standard:** Use AgentSkills.io for portability across global marketplaces.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Reach:** Traditional services are local. Digital services are global.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **Multi-Region Deployment:** Using Kubernetes to spin up agent nodes close to the user (Latency optimization).

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Is your agent 'Locked' to your website, or can it live in the user's workflow?*
