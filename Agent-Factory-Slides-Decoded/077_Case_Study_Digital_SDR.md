# Slide 77: Case Study: The "Digital SDR" Agent

## Core Message
**Automating the Top of the Funnel**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Scenario
*   **Problem:** Human SDRs burn out, miss follow-ups, and have low volume (50/day).
*   **Solution:** Digital SDR.
    *   **Skill:** `sales-expert` (Qualification logic).
    *   **Tools:** LinkedIn, Gmail, CRM.

#### 2. Why it works
High ROI, high repetitiveness. The "Perfect" use case.

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To provide a concrete "Proof of Concept" that everyone understands. Sales = Money.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** It emails people for you.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **Hyper-Personalization at Scale.** A human can personalize 10 emails a day. An agent can personalize 1,000 emails a day by reading the prospect's LinkedIn, recent news, and company report *in real time* before writing the email.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** Mail merge (Not an agent).
*   **Intermediate:** Agent that inserts "Company Name" and "Job Title."
*   **PhD / Advanced:** Agent that notices a prospect just raised Series B, congratulates them on the specific round size, references their hiring plan, and maps your product to their new growth goals.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Stack:** `Apollo.io` (Data) -> `Claude` (Writer) -> `Gmail` (Sender).

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Revenue:** It directly drives the topline.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Lead Database:** State management for "Contacted," "Replied," "Meeting Booked."

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you using AI to spam (bad) or to personalize (good)?*
