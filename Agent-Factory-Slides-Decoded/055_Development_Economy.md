### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To quantify the economic shock. $3 Trillion is a massive number. It implies that every software budget in the world is about to change.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Software gets cheaper.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **Deflationary Pressure.** The cost of writing code is collapsing. This means the *supply* of software will explode. We will have software for niche problems that were previously too small to address (e.g., "A CRM for dog walkers in Austin"). This is the **Long Tail of Software**.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** A $50k app now costs $5k.
*   **Intermediate:** A 6-month project now takes 2 weeks.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Hyper-Personalized Software.** Every employee gets a custom dashboard built *just for them* by an agent, optimized for their specific workflow. The concept of "Standard Corporate Software" dies.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Metric:** Cost per Feature.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Budget reallocation:** Money saved on *coding* will be spent on *compute* and *strategy*.
*   **Survival:** Dev shops charging by the hour for manual coding will go bankrupt.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The "Software Supply Chain":** Moving from "Artisanal Craft" to "Factory Production."

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you selling 'Hours of Code' (Depreciating) or 'Business Outcomes' (Appreciating)?*
