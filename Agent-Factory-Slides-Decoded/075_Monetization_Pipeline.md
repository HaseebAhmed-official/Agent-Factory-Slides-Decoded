# Slide 75: The "Skill-First" Monetization Pipeline

## Core Message
**Liquidating Expertise: The 3-Phase Path to Revenue**

### 1. Objective
To provide a systematic process for turning human brainpower into a scalable digital asset. This slide is the "Assembly Line" for expertise.

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **Knowledge is Currently Illiquid:** Your expertise is trapped in your head. You can only sell it one hour at a time (Consulting).
*   **Hardening is Mandatory:** A prompt is "Soft." A Skill folder (`SKILL.md` + Tools) is "Hard." Hard assets are sellable; soft ones are not.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights
1.  **Phase 1: Knowledge Extraction:** Record yourself or an expert doing the job. Document every rule, edge case, and intuition.
2.  **Phase 2: Asset Hardening:** Transform that messy knowledge into a **Structured Spec** and a modular **Agent Skill folder**. Build the tests.
3.  **Phase 3: Deployment & Capture:** Host the agent, connect it to the client's data (MCP), and start billing for the outcome.

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **Knowledge Engineering:** Phase 1 is the bottleneck. It requires "Knowledge Engineering"—the art of interviewing an expert to reveal the **"Tacit Knowledge"** they don't even know they have (e.g., "I just *know* this invoice looks fake").
*   **Asset Hardening (The "Compile" Phase):** This is where you move from "Vibe" to "Code." You use a General Agent to write the Python scripts that enforce the expert's rules. This turns "Opinions" into "Algorithms."
*   **Value Capture:** In Phase 3, you choose your "Capture" method. Do you sell a subscription? Or do you take a "Success Fee"? (Slide 64).

#### 4. When to Use?
*   **Consulting-to-Product Pivot:** When a service company wants to stop selling hours and start selling software.
*   **New Product Discovery:** When identifying which business area to automate next.

#### 5. Examples

##### a. Basic (The 'Book' model)
*   *Extraction:* Write a blog post.
*   *Hardening:* Turn it into a PDF.
*   *Capture:* Sell for $10. (Lower value, no agency).

##### b. Intermediate (The 'Agent' model)
*   *Extraction:* Record a video of yourself auditing an SEO report.
*   *Hardening:* Build an "SEO Auditor Skill" with Python scripts to check backlinks.
*   *Capture:* Sell for $500/mo.

##### c. PhD / Advanced (The 'Expert Factory')
*   *Concept:* **Knowledge Arbitrage Swarm.**
*   *Scenario:* You find the top 10 tax lawyers in the country. You use the pipeline to extract their collective wisdom into a "Supreme Tax Agent." You "Harden" it with thousands of edge cases and formal verification. You then "Deploy" it to 5,000 accounting firms globally. You have **Liquidated the world's top expertise** and distributed it at zero marginal cost.

#### 6. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Toolchain:** `Loom` (Extraction) -> `Claude` (Drafting Spec) -> `Spec Kit Plus` (Hardening) -> `Docker` (Deployment).

#### 7. Why This Matters?
*   **Scalability:** You can't scale a guru. You can scale a file.
*   **Wealth Building:** Assets build wealth; labor builds income. This pipeline builds assets.

#### 8. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **The "Consultant's Trap":** Prevents experts from being limited by the 24 hours in a day.

#### 9. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Hardening Layer:** The most important part is the **TDD/Eval** suite (Slide 63). If it's not tested, it's not "Hardened."

#### 10. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Skipping Phase 1 and trying to "guess" the rules.
    *   *Correction:* Always record a real human doing the work first.
*   **Practice:** "The 80/20 Rule." Extract the 20% of rules that handle 80% of the cases first.
