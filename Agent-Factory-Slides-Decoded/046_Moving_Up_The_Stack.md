# Slide 46: Moving Up The Stack

## Core Message
**Where is the Value? Competing at the Skill Layer.**

### 1. Objective
To guide business strategy. Identifying where "Alpha" (excess return) comes from in the AI era. It shifts focus from "Building Models" to "Building Skills."

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **The Commoditization of Intelligence:** Intelligence (LLMs) is becoming like electricity—cheap, ubiquitous, and standardized.
*   **The Scarcity of Expertise:** Domain expertise (Law, Medicine, Specific Business Logic) remains scarce. The value accrues to the layer that *encapsulates* this scarcity.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights
*   **Models (Bottom):** Commodity. Everyone has GPT-4. Hard to differentiate.
*   **Agents (Middle):** Emerging Standard. The runtime.
*   **Skills (Top):** **Proprietary Logic.** The Winning Layer. This is where you put your secret sauce.

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **The "Thin Wrapper" Problem:** Apps that just "wrap" GPT-4 are dead. Apps that wrap **Unique Data + Unique Workflows (Skills)** are defensible.
*   **The "Skill" as Capital:** The Skill folder is the crystallization of your business process. It is an asset that generates cash flow.

### 4. When to Use?
*   **Strategic Planning:** Deciding what to build.
*   **Investor Pitching:** Explaining why your AI startup won't be killed by OpenAI. ("We own the Skills, they just own the Model").

### 5. Examples

#### a. Basic (Commodity)
*   *Product:* A "Chat with PDF" app. (Anyone can build this).

#### b. Intermediate (Better)
*   *Product:* A "Legal Contract Reviewer" with proprietary prompts and templates.

#### c. PhD / Advanced (Monopoly)
*   *Concept:* **Networked Skill Ecosystem.**
*   *Scenario:* You build a "Network of Agents" that simulates a specific market sector (e.g., Real Estate in Dubai) using proprietary historical data and behavioral skills. This simulation capability is a monopoly because no one else has the *combination* of Data + Logic (Skills) to replicate it.

### 6. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Focus Allocation:** Spend 80% of time on `SKILL.md` / `tools`, 20% on infrastructure.

### 7. Why This Matters?
*   **Investment:** Investors want to know "What is your Moat?" The answer is "My Library of Specialized Skills."
*   **Survival:** If you compete on models, you lose to Big Tech. If you compete on Skills, you win.

### 8. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **The "Feature vs. Product" Debate:** Skills turn AI features into defensible Products.

### 9. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **Abstraction Layers:** We abstract away the Model so we can focus on the Logic. The architecture should allow swapping the "Engine" (Model) without breaking the "Car" (Skill).

### 10. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Focusing on Fine-tuning models.
    *   *Correction:* Fine-tuning is hard to maintain. RAG + Skills is easier and often better.
*   **Practice:** "Domain First." Start with the business problem, not the AI tech.

### 11. Reflection Questions
1.  *Are you building a commodity (Wrapper) or a proprietary asset (Skill)?*
2.  *If GPT-5 comes out, does your business die or get stronger? (Should be stronger).*
