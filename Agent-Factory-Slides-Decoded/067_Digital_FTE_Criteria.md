# Slide 67: What defines a Digital FTE?

## Core Message
**The Criteria for a Digital Employee: Beyond the Bot**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Digital FTE Checklist
To be considered a "Full-Time Equivalent" (FTE) and not just a "Script," an agent must meet these five criteria:
1.  **Defined Role:** It has a clear job title (e.g., "Social Media Manager") and a specific set of responsibilities.
2.  **Uses Tools/APIs:** It can "do work" on other systems (Slack, Jira, QuickBooks, Browser).
3.  **Operates Continuously:** It doesn't wait for a human to click "Run." It monitors its environment 24/7.
4.  **Predictable Cost:** You know exactly what it costs to have this worker "on staff" per month.
5.  **Handles Ambiguity:** It can figure out 90% of the "How" when given the "What." It solves problems autonomously.

#### 2. The "FTE" Litmus Test
"If you can't describe what the agent does as a 'Job Role' that you would hire a person for on LinkedIn, it's not a Digital FTE. It's just a feature."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To distinguish between "Task Automation" (SaaS) and "Role Automation" (Agentic AI). This slide defines the "Widget" that the Agent Factory produces.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation

##### a. Basic Insights
*   **A Teammate, Not a Tool:** You *use* Excel; you *manage* an Accountant. An FTE is a managed entity.
*   **Proactivity:** An FTE says, "I noticed the budget was high, so I adjusted the campaign." A tool waits for you to check the budget.

##### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **The "Accountability" Layer:** A true Digital FTE must be able to report on its own performance. It needs an "Audit Trail" that maps its actions to business goals. If it can't explain *why* it did something, it lacks the professional maturity of an FTE.
*   **Social Integration:** An FTE needs a "Presence." This means it has its own email address, its own Slack handle, and its own permissions in the system. It is a **First-Class Citizen** of the corporate network.
*   **Recursive Self-Correction:** A Digital FTE should have a "Self-Eval" skill. Every night at 2:00 AM, it should review its own logs from the day, identify mistakes, and update its own `SKILL.md` to prevent those mistakes tomorrow. This is the definition of a "Professional."

#### 3. When to Use?
*   **Product Definition:** When deciding the scope of your agentic product.
*   **Performance Reviews:** Auditing your digital workforce to see if they are meeting the "FTE standard."

#### 4. Examples

##### a. Basic (The 'Bot')
*   *Action:* A script that summarizes a meeting when you upload the recording. (Fail: Not an FTE, just a feature).

##### b. Intermediate (The 'FTE')
*   *Role:* "Meeting Coordinator."
*   *Action:* It joins every Zoom meeting automatically, takes notes, assigns Jira tickets, follow-ups with humans who haven't replied, and manages the team calendar. (Pass: Meets all 5 criteria).

##### c. PhD / Advanced (The 'Digital Executive')
*   *Concept:* **Executive Agency.**
*   *Scenario:* A "Digital VP of Growth." It has its own budget (in a company credit card). it monitors the CPA (Cost per Acquisition) of 10 different ad channels. It autonomously moves money from underperforming channels to winning ones. It interviews and "hires" other specialized "Copywriter Agents" from a marketplace. It only emails the CEO once a week with a profit report.

#### 5. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Feature:** Persistent background processes (Daemons) and long-term memory (Vector DB).

#### 6. Why This Matters?
*   **Pricing Power:** You can charge $2,000/mo for an FTE. You can only charge $20/mo for a tool.

#### 7. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **The "Micromanager" Trap:** Prevents managers from having to spend all day prompting AI. The FTE handles the sub-steps.

#### 8. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Persistent State Machine:** An FTE requires a system that maintains state across months, not just minutes.

#### 9. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Building an agent that is too broad.
    *   *Correction:* Give it a narrow job title. "Digital LinkedIn Lead Scraper" is better than "Digital Sales Person."
*   **Practice:** "Job Descriptions." Write a real HR Job Description for your agent before you write the first line of code.

#### 10. Reflection Questions
1.  *Does your agent have a 'Manager'? (If not, who is responsible when it fails?)*
2.  *Can your agent 'fire' its own tools if they are too expensive?*
3.  *If your agent was a human, would you promote them after 6 months?*
