### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

To give the audience homework. Remove the excuse of "I don't know where to start."

*   **Advanced Insights:** **Time-Boxing.** The 30-day limit forces you to cut scope. You can't build "AGI" in 30 days. You *can* build an "Invoice Reader." This enforces the **MVA (Minimum Viable Agent)** discipline.

#### 3. Examples

*   **Week 2:** Write `SKILL.md`.
*   **Week 3:** Run `claude` to write the script.
*   **Week 4:** Put it on a server and invoice the client.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
