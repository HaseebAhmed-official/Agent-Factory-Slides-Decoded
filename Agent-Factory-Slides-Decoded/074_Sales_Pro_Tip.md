# Slide 74: Pro-Tip for Sales Presentations

## Core Message
**Selling the Cost per Task**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Strategy
Don't talk about "AI." Talk about **Task Economics**.

#### 2. The Data
*   **Human Cost per Task:** ~$5.00.
*   **Agent Cost per Task:** **~$0.50**.

#### 3. The Logic
"When you show a CFO a 90% cost reduction per task, the sale is automatic."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To give a tactical sales weapon.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Money talks.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **Granularity.** Most companies don't know their "Cost per Task." By helping them calculate it (e.g., "How much does it cost to process *one* invoice?"), you position yourself as a consultant, then sell the solution.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** "Our AI is cheap."
*   **Intermediate:** "It costs you $10 to process an email. We do it for $1."
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Gain Share.** "We will do it for $2 (saving you $8). We keep $1 of the savings."

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Dashboard:** Show "Money Saved" ticker on the agent dashboard.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Conversion:** CFOs approve savings. CTOs approve tech. Sell to the CFO.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Ledger:** Every agent action logs a "Cost" event to calculate this metric in real-time.

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Do you know your customer's 'Unit Economics'?*
