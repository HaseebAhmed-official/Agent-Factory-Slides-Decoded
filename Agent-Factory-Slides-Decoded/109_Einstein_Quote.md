# Slide 109: Einstein Quote

## Core Message
**"There comes a time we need to stop reading the books of others. And write our own."**

### 1. Objective
To provide emotional and philosophical closure. To move the audience from "Passive Consumption" to "Active Creation."

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **Intellectual Sovereignty:** In the age of LLMs, it's easy to let the "Model" do the thinking for you. Einstein's quote is a warning against **Cognitive Dependency**.
*   **The Maker's Ethos:** To win in the Agentic era, you must use the AI to express *your* unique vision and expertise. The "Books" you are writing are the `SKILL.md` files that define your legacy.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights
*   **Stop Reading:** Stop consuming hype.
*   **Start Writing:** Start building agents.

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **The "Blank Page" Opportunity:** The rules of the Agentic Era haven't been written yet. You are not late; you are early. You have the chance to define the standards (like `AGENTS.md`) that the rest of the world will use.

### 4. When to Use?
*   **Motivation:** When you feel overwhelmed by how much there is to learn.
*   **Decision Making:** "Should I buy a course or build a project?" (Build the project).
