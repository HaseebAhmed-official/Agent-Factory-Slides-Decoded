# Slide 43: Trends in Skills ecosystem

## Core Message
**The Maturation of Agentic AI: Production-Grade Standards**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. Major Trends
*   **Production-Grade Focus:** Moving from demos to 24/7 reliability.
*   **Complementing MCP:** Skills provide the "Brain," MCP provides the "Hands."
*   **Non-Developers:** SMEs (Lawyers, Doctors) building skills.
*   **Repositories:** Marketplaces like AgentSkills.io.

#### 2. The "Universal Driver"
"Agent Skills are standardizing how we connect **expertise** to any AI model."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To analyze where the market is going. We are moving from the "Wild West" of prompting to standardized "Engineering."

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Skills are getting serious. They are being built by experts, not just techies.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The Rise of the SME-Engineer.** The person who knows *Law* best is the best person to write the *Legal Skill*, provided the format is simple (Markdown). This democratizes software creation to non-engineers.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** A "Joke Writing Skill" on GitHub.
*   **Intermediate:** A "HIPAA Compliance Skill" sold by a healthcare consultancy.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Certified Skills.** A "GAAP Accounting Skill" that is digitally signed by the AICPA (American Institute of CPAs), guaranteeing its correctness.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Ecosystem:** The separation of "Skill Builders" (SMEs) and "Platform Builders" (Techies).

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Trust:** Standardization builds trust.
*   **Network Effects:** A standard format allows skills to be shared/sold globally.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Skill Registry:** A centralized or decentralized index of available skills (like `npm` or `PyPI` for Agents).

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you building skills that are robust enough to be sold?*
