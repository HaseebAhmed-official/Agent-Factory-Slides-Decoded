# Slide 90: Pricing & Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

## Core Message
**How to Price Digital Labor: Extreme Margins**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. Pricing Tiers
*   **Starter Role ($500 - $2,000 / mo):** Simple tasks (Scraping, sorting).
*   **Specialist Role ($2,000 - $5,000 / mo):** Domain knowledge (Accounting, Support).
*   **Enterprise Role ($10,000+ / mo):** Complex orchestration, high security.

#### 2. The Economics
*   **Token Costs:** 10-20% of price.
*   **Hosting/Ops:** 5-10% of price.
*   **Net Margin:** **70-85%.**

#### 3. The "Aha!" Moment
"Selling $60k/year human value for $12k/year, costing you $1k/year to run. Everyone wins."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To establish the "Unit Economics" of a Digital FTE business.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Charge a lot, spend a little.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **Value-Based Pricing vs Cost-Plus Pricing.** Traditional software is priced "Cost-Plus" ($10/mo). Digital Labor is priced "Value-Based" (relative to a salary). This allows you to capture the **Consumer Surplus** created by AI. **Token Arbitrage.** As token costs drop (Moores Law for LLMs), your margins *expand* automatically if you keep your prices stable.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** $20/mo tool.
*   **Intermediate:** $1,500/mo "Digital Paralegal."
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Dynamic ROI Pricing.** An agent that monitors its own "Value Created" (e.g., money recovered in an audit) and bills the client 15% of that value automatically via a smart contract.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Billing:** Use Stripe "Subscriptions" with "Usage Metering."

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Profitability:** This is why Agentic AI is the "Greatest Business Opportunity." The margins are higher than SaaS.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Billing Sidecar:** A module that calculates the "Cost of Goods Sold" (COGS) for every agent run.

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Are you pricing like a 'Utility' or a 'Partner'?*
*   *Do you know your profit margin per agent?*
