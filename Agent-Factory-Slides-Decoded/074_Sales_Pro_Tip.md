# Slide 74: Sales Pro-Tip: Task Economics

## Core Message
**The CFO's "Yes": Selling Cost per Task, Not AI Hype**

### 1. Objective
To provide a tactical sales weapon for Agentic Engineers. This slide moves the conversation from "Technology" to "Unit Economics," making the purchase decision a mathematical "No-brainer."

### 2. Critical Analysis & Rationale
*   **The Anti-Hype Strategy:** Enterprise buyers are tired of "AI." They want "Savings."
*   **Granularity:** By breaking work into "Tasks," you make the ROI auditable and indisputable on a spreadsheet.

### 3. Step-by-Step Explanation

#### a. Basic Insights
*   **The Strategy:** Don't talk about LLMs or Models. Talk about the **Cost per Task**.
*   **The Comparison:**
    *   **Human Cost per Task:** ~$5.00 (Includes salary, taxes, office, benefits, and coffee breaks).
    *   **Agent Cost per Task:** **~$0.50** (Includes API tokens and hosting).

#### b. Advanced Insights (Deeper Look)
*   **Unit Economic Dominance:** A 90% reduction in per-task costs doesn't just "save money"—it allows for **New Business Models**. If it costs $5 to audit an invoice, you only audit $1,000 invoices. If it costs $0.50, you audit **every** $10 invoice. You increase coverage and quality while reducing cost.
*   **The "Shadow Cost" of Humans:** Humans have a high "Switching Cost" and "Onboarding Cost." Agents have near-zero. When selling, include the cost of **management time** in the human side of the ledger.
*   **Token Deflation:** As models get cheaper (GPT-4 to GPT-4o-mini), the agent cost drops further. Your margins *expand* over time if you keep your prices stable.

#### 4. When to Use?
*   **CFO Meetings:** When justifying the "Pilot Project."
*   **Pricing Design:** When determining your own margins.

#### 5. Examples

##### a. Basic (The Invoice Audit)
*   *Human:* $5.00.
*   *Agent:* $0.50.
*   *Sales Pitch:* "We will audit all 10,000 of your monthly invoices for the price of 1,000."

##### b. Intermediate (The Customer Support Ticket)
*   *Traditional:* $15.00 per resolution.
*   *Digital FTE:* $1.50 per resolution.
*   *ROI:* The support department moves from a "Cost Center" to a "Strategic Advantage."

##### c. PhD / Advanced (Outcome-Based Gain Share)
*   *Concept:* **Contingency Labor Pricing.**
*   *Scenario:* An agent performs "Utility Bill Auditing."
*   *Deal:* You charge $0 upfront. The agent finds $100,000 in overcharges. You keep 20% ($20,000). Your token cost was $50. Your ROI is **40,000%**. This is only possible when the cost-per-task is so low that you can afford to work on "Success Only" models.

#### 6. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Dashboarding:** Build a "Savings Ticker" into the agent's control panel.

#### 7. Why This Matters?
*   **The "Yes" velocity:** CFOs approve $50k "Efficiency Projects" in one meeting. They take 6 months to approve "AI Transformation" projects.

#### 8. What Problem Does It Solve?
*   **The "Value Gap":** Developers often struggle to explain why their "Cool Bot" is worth $2,000/month. This slide provides the math.

#### 9. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **Cost-Aware Routing:** The architecture should route simple tasks to cheap models to ensure the $0.50 target is met.

#### 10. Common Practices & Pitfalls
*   **Pitfall:** Forgetting to include the "Human Management" cost of the agent.
    *   *Correction:* Always include a 5-10% buffer for human oversight.
*   **Practice:** Start with the "Most Expensive Human Task" to show the biggest ROI first.

#### 11. Reflection Questions
1.  *What is the most expensive repetitive task in your company today?*
2.  *Could you do that task for 1/10th the cost?*
3.  *Who is the 'Economic Buyer' at your target company? (Hint: It's the person who hates the $5.00 bill).*
