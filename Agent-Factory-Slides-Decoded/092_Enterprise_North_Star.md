# Slide 92: Enterprise North Star: The Digital FTE

## Core Message
**Building Teammates, Not Just Apps**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Objective
The "North Star" for any factory implementation is to create a worker that feels like a human teammate.

#### 2. Characteristics
*   **Initiative:** It doesn't wait for prompts.
*   **Context:** It knows the company's past.
*   **Accountability:** It reports on its successes and failures.

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To align the development team with the "User Experience" of an employee.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** Make it useful, not just smart.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The "Teammate" UX.** A teammate doesn't just give you a block of text. A teammate says: "I noticed X, so I did Y, and I need you to approve Z." This is **Proactive Communication**. The transition from "Reactive Chatbot" to "Proactive Teammate" is the defining UX challenge of 2026.

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** A bot that answers when asked.
*   **Intermediate:** A bot that emails you a summary of the day at 5 PM.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **Agentic Presence.** An agent that has its own Slack handle, participates in "Stand-up" meetings by posting its progress, and "Asks for help" when it's blocked by a technical issue. It has a "Social Identity" in the company.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Feature:** Proactive triggers (Webhooks).

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Retention:** You don't cancel a teammate.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Event Bus:** The agent "listens" to the company's digital pulse (Slack, Git, Jira) and acts when it sees an opportunity to help.

#### 7. Reflection Questions
*   *Does your agent have a job title?*
*   *Does it have a Slack account?*
