# Slide 66: Our Vision

## Core Message
**"Our vision is to create Digital Full-Time Equivalents (Digital FTEs) that perform real, repeatable knowledge work. These are structured AI workers, designed for specific functions, distinct from generic, conversational AI."**

The visual depicts a complex, interconnected circuit board or city-plan, symbolizing that a Digital FTE is not a single script, but an orchestrated system of multiple components (Skills, MCPs, Logic) working in unison.

---

## 1. Strategic Breakdown

### Structured vs. Generic
*   **Generic AI (ChatGPT):** Designed for conversation. It is a "Chatbot." It is unstructured and reactive.
*   **Structured AI (Digital FTE):** Designed for **function**. It is a "Worker." It is proactive and follows a strict architectural blueprint.

### Real, Repeatable Knowledge Work
The vision focuses on "Drudgery"—high-volume, boring, but critical tasks.
*   *Example:* Invoice Processing, Lead Qualification, Log Analysis.
*   *Goal:* To turn this variable human labor into a fixed, reliable software asset.

---

## 2. Technical Context (The Circuit Metaphor)

The diagram on the slide represents the **Architecture-First** approach.
*   **Central Hub:** The Core Logic / Orchestrator (General Agent).
*   **Peripheral Nodes:** The specific **Skills** (folders) and **MCP Servers** (tools) plugged into the core.
*   **Pathways:** The flow of data and control between the brain and the hands.

This vision sets the stage for the **Factory Model**: We aren't painting art; we are engineering reliable machinery.
