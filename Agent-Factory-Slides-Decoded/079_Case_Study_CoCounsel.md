## Core Message
**The $650 Million "Seat" Model**

### Detailed Analysis (Original Context)

#### 1. The Story
*   **Role:** Digital Legal Assistant.
*   **Task:** Document review, memo drafting.
*   **Price:** $500/month/seat.
*   **Exit:** Acquired for $650M by Thomson Reuters.

#### 2. The Lesson
"They didn't invent GPT-4. They built the **Legal Skills** on top of it."

---

### Strategic Deep Dive (GEMINI.md Extensions)

#### 1. Objective
To show "Unicorn" potential. This isn't just small automation; it's enterprise value creation.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Explanation
*   **Basic Insights:** AI for Lawyers.
*   **Advanced Insights:** **The "Trust" Premium.** Lawyers bill at $500/hr. If an AI can do the work, it is worth a lot. But it must be *trusted*. CoCounsel succeeded because they focused on **Accuracy** and **Security** (No hallucinations, no data leaks).

#### 3. Examples
*   **Basic:** ChatGPT summarizing a contract.
*   **Intermediate:** A specialized prompt for contracts.
*   **PhD / Advanced:** **RAG over Case Law.** CoCounsel indexes millions of legal cases and cross-references them to ensure the "Memo" cites real precedents.

#### 4. Implementation in Agentic AI
*   **Vertical AI:** Pick a high-value vertical (Law, Medicine) and go deep.

#### 5. Why This Matters?
*   **Valuation:** Vertical Agents command massive valuations.

#### 6. Architecture Deep Dive
*   **The Citation Engine:** A specific module that verifies every claim against a source document.
